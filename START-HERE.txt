KH 0.2 Archipelago | Steam | Public beta

Install kh02.apworld from Archipelago Launcher and restart the launcher.
If you already have a KH 0.2 APWorld, keep a copy of your old download.

In Steam, browse the local files for Kingdom Hearts HD 2.8 Final Chapter
Prologue. Open the KINGDOM HEARTS 0.2 Birth by Sleep folder. You should see
Binaries and Content inside. With the game closed, extract kh02-steam-mod.zip
here so those two folders merge with the existing folders. If Windows asks
to replace an existing file, cancel and check which mod owns that file.
Keep this zip as your list of AP files.

Generate a player YAML in Archipelago's Options Creator and include it when
generating your seed, or use the room your host provides. For tracking, put
kh02-poptracker-0.4.13.zip in PopTracker's packs folder and connect it to
the same room and slot.

Sign in to Steam. Open KH 0.2 Client from Archipelago Launcher and connect
with your server address and player name. Wait for "AP is prepared" before
launching the collection. Select 0.2 and wait at title for "AP is attached".
For a new seed, choose New Game. On later visits, reopen the KH client,
connect to the same room and player slot, wait for attachment, then load
your AP save. If your vanilla saves appear, close the game before playing.

Close the game before closing the client. The mod stays installed between AP
sessions. Closing the client alone does NOT disable the mod.

SWITCHING TO VANILLA

1. Close KH 0.2 normally, let the client finish cleanup, then close the client.
2. Open the KINGDOM HEARTS 0.2 Birth by Sleep folder inside the Steam install.
3. Make a backup folder OUTSIDE the game folder, such as "KH02 AP disabled"
   on your Desktop. Inside it, make two folders: "paks" and "loaders".
4. Move these two files from Content\Paks\~mods into your backup's paks folder:
   - KH02_AP_RestartPrototype_P.pak
   - TresGame-WindowsNoEditor_99_P.pak
5. Move these two files from Binaries\Win64 into your backup's loaders folder:
   - dinput8.dll
   - dwmapi.dll
6. Launch the game normally, without opening the KH client. Your vanilla
   saves should appear.

Move the files; don't delete them. Leave all saves, AP profiles, and other
files alone. Moving only the paks does not disable the native AP loaders.
These instructions assume the loaders came from this AP mod; if another mod
shares them, disabling them may disable that mod too.

TO PLAY AP AGAIN

With the game closed, move the two paks back into Content\Paks\~mods and the
two loaders back into Binaries\Win64. If a file already exists there, don't
overwrite it blindly. Then open a fresh KH client, connect to your seed, and
wait for "AP is prepared" before launching. Your seed's AP saves are retained.

The client keeps AP saves and receipt history in the seed's profile under
%LOCALAPPDATA%/KH02AP. It creates temporary session files beside the game
and stages its native transport under %LOCALAPPDATA%/KH02AP/runtime.

REMOVING OLD SEED SAVES

New seeds add profile folders; reconnecting to the same seed reuses its
folder. The client never deletes old profiles automatically.

1. Close the game normally, wait for cleanup, then close the KH client.
2. Paste %LOCALAPPDATA%\KH02AP\profiles into File Explorer's address bar.
3. Folder names start with the seed name, then the player slot (such as P1)
   and an identifier. Open profile.json in Notepad and check its label for
   the seed and player name. Don't edit it.
4. Move the ENTIRE old seed folder elsewhere to keep a backup, or delete
   that folder if you no longer want its local progress.

Keep saves and receipt history together. Don't delete individual files
inside a profile to troubleshoot a run you want to keep. This does not
remove server-side checks, vanilla saves, or other seed profiles.
To resume a backup, restore the whole folder to its original location with
the game and client closed. Don't merge it with a newly created profile.

In-game difficulty changes: enter /difficulty beginner, standard, proud or
critical in the KH client (for example, /difficulty proud), then reload your
AP save. Use /difficulty status to see the selected setting.
