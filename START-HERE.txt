Kingdom Hearts 0.2 — Archipelago Steam public beta

For the Windows Steam version of Kingdom Hearts HD 2.8 Final Chapter Prologue.
Epic Games Store support is planned separately.

SETUP

1. Install kh02.apworld through Archipelago, then restart the Archipelago Launcher.
2. Generate a Kingdom Hearts 0.2 YAML with Archipelago's Options Creator. Choose
   your settings and include it when generating your multiworld. Existing seeds
   retain their original item placements and generation rules.
3. Put kh02-poptracker-0.4.11.zip in PopTracker's packs folder and select it.
   Connect PopTracker to your AP room when you want automatic tracking.

PLAY

1. Open Steam and sign into the account you use for the game. Keep 0.2 closed.
2. Open "KH 0.2 Client" from the Archipelago Launcher.
3. Connect using your AP server address and player name.
4. Wait for "AP is prepared" before launching 2.8 and selecting 0.2.
5. Wait for the client to attach, then start a new game or load this seed's AP save.

The client includes and temporarily installs the required mod. You do not need
to install a separate mod or enter your Steam ID. AP saves are kept separately
from vanilla saves and other seeds. If you see your vanilla saves, stop and check
that the client prepared and attached before loading anything.

END A SESSION

Close the game normally, then close the client. The client restores normal
startup automatically; you do not need to remove the mod manually.

If you close the client first, it waits for the game to close before finishing
cleanup. Close the game normally and let the client finish. Avoid force-ending
the client. If cleanup reports an error, retain the message and recovery files.

Open a fresh KH client window for each new game session. Reconnecting the old
window is not enough to prepare another launch.

USEFUL COMMANDS

/help
/difficulty status
/difficulty beginner
/difficulty standard
/difficulty proud
/difficulty critical

After choosing a difficulty, reload your AP save for the change to apply.

PUBLIC TESTING

This build includes the completed seeded playthrough fixes. Please report your
AP settings, location, what happened, what you expected, and any client errors.
For problems after loading or traveling, include the save slot and destination.

Mickey's movement outside Depths can be imperfect. He can catch up by warping,
and may remain outside some boss barriers while still assisting. This behavior
depends on the Mickey option you selected.

Player downloads: kh02.apworld, PopTracker 0.4.11, and these instructions.
Archipelago 0.6.7 was used for the installed-client acceptance test.
