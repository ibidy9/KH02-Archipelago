# Objective catalog

Source: [Destiny Islands — All Objectives](https://www.destinyislands.com/kh-02-bbs/side-quests/objectives/)
(accessed 2026-08-15).

Cross-check: [PlayStation Universe — Kingdom Hearts 0.2 Objectives Guide](https://www.psu.com/news/kingdom-hearts-0-2-objectives-guide-complete-all-challenges/)
(published 2017-02-13; accessed 2026-08-15).

This table preserves the source's objective number, name, short requirement,
availability condition, location, and wardrobe reward. Requirements are
condensed rather than copied verbatim. The availability column is especially
important for Archipelago generation logic.

Destiny Islands has an internal inconsistency for Objective 13: its short line
says 30 lightning defeats, while its detailed explanation says 50. PSU also
transcribes the in-game requirement as 50, so this catalog uses 50.

cleared file, while five require or are best handled by starting a new game
with completion data. Destiny Islands labels all sixteen as starting over clear
data, so the exact minimum route still needs in-game validation.

## Archipelago pool policy

| Pool | Initial policy |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed exploration, puzzle, and encounter objectives from 1-35 | Primary default candidates after flag validation |
| Ability-dependent objectives | Require the corresponding Magic, Doubleflight, Shotlock, or Spellweaver item in logic |
| Enemy-count and level objectives | Optional grind pool |
| Objectives 36-50 | Clear-data/NG+ option only |
| Objective 51 | Clear-data plus Critical-only option with client validation |

No objective becomes an Archipelago location until its completion flag is
detectable and the in-game requirement has been verified on the Steam build.
