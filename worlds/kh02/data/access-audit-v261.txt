KH 0.2 access-rule review — 2026-10-16, v261

Scope
All 142 catalog checks, both goals, item classification and placement rules.
This is an offline logic correction, not new proof of gameplay behavior.
No native runtime, game asset, save or server seed is modified.
Earlier generation rules included unsupported restrictions. They were not
evidence of a gameplay requirement and have been replaced as described below.

Single source
world-source/kh02/data/access-rules-v261.json defines every check and goal.
tools/build_access_rules_v261.py exports the same expressions to PopTracker.
The APWorld and tracker evaluate these expressions independently. Generation
uses seed-selected progression wardrobe sets/pieces; the tracker intentionally
recognizes any actual qualifying wardrobe combination, matching the game.

Traversal
No chest receives a movement requirement because its vanilla reward is a
Zodiac. All Zodiac chests use their world's access, per the user's correction.
Doubleflight applies to WW hub chest 2, regular Mines chests 7/8, Mines mirror,
Phantom 3, WW completion and collecting every WW chest. It does not apply to
Phantoms 1/2 or the non-Mines mirrors. Air Slide is not a blanket requirement.
Forest chest 4 remains in generator logic without Doubleflight; the tracker
shows yellow without it because the alternate route is exceptionally hard.
Castle traversal/high point and Forest ivy-rail objective use the proven
movement-free routes. Lingering Memories use world access without movement
items, following the user's recollection; this was not newly live-tested.
Evidence: docs/handoff-ready-2026-09-07.md, research/location-catalog.md,
docs/movement-lock-run.md (earlier conservative entries superseded by handoff).

Zodiac Phantom and goals
User's green tracker kit: Cartwheel + Barrier + Curaga. Yellow if any is
missing, but only after world and entrance requirements are satisfied.
Generation requires Cartwheel + Barrier; the prior user instruction allows
Cure-free completion but forbids placing Cure behind the final Phantom.
Cure is excluded from round 5 and Objective 42 even when important rewards
there are enabled. See docs/zodiac-phantom-defensive-logic-2026-09-24.md.
The first four rush rounds no longer require the entire loadout. Ordinary
bosses and Demon Tide have no unsupported all-spells/all-abilities gate.
Difficulty selection and renewable grinding are available; this is not a
guarantee that every player can win with minimal equipment.

Placement/classification
Mickey is progression when randomized: his two objectives depend on him.
World access remains excluded from level checks after 60.
Zodiac self-lock prevention and the optional final-Phantom important-reward
setting remain. Item/location IDs and slot-data schema are unchanged.

Presentation and reliability
Yellow individual checks: Forest chest 4, final Zodiac Phantom, Objective 41
without Cartwheel/Barrier, and Objective 47 without Barrier. World access
and Doubleflight for reaching Phantom 3 still apply before yellow is shown.
Unknown or inaccessible checks remain red. Accepted layout/art are unchanged.
The prior label cleanup and WW tab rename are included. Client AP status is
printed only when its state changes, rather than on each observation.
